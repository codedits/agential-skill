## 1. Futuristic Personal OS Hero

![Futuristic Personal OS Hero](../resources/design-references/section-hero-futuristic-os.webp)

- **Height:** `min-height: 100vh` (desktop) · `min-height: 100svh` (mobile)
- **Signature component:** Floating glassmorphic mode-switcher badge with high-contrast headline and luminous accent lighting.
- **Patterns:** Semi-transparent pill tags with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Minimal CTA radius (6px). Floating status pill.
- **When to use:** Next-gen SaaS, AI interfaces, hardware product launches.

## 2. Editorial Architectural Grid

![Editorial Architectural Grid](../resources/design-references/section-portfolio-editorial-grid.webp)

- **Height:** `min-height: 130–140vh` (desktop) · `min-height: auto` (mobile)
- **Signature component:** Massive display typography intersecting a central model cutout, surrounded by architectural crosshair grid and 4-column metadata bar.
- **Patterns:** 1px hairline border grid with coordinate markers. Generous vertical padding (`120px 0`). Staggered bio card with accent frame.
- **When to use:** Creative agency portfolios, architecture firms, luxury consultancy.

## 3. Brutalist Halftone & Kinetic Typography

![Brutalist Halftone & Kinetic Typography](../resources/design-references/section-brutalist-kinetic-type.webp)

- **Height:** `min-height: 120–130vh` (desktop) · `min-height: auto` (mobile)
- **Signature component:** High-contrast halftone textures mixed with 3D mechanical keycaps and rhythmic condensed kinetic typography.
- **Patterns:** Black, cream, and vermilion palette. Tactile 3D skeuomorphic keycaps. QR widget and studio badge metadata.
- **When to use:** Design studios, streetwear, experimental brands.

## 4. Luxury E-Commerce Headline Overlay

![Luxury E-Commerce Headline Overlay](../resources/design-references/section-hero-luxury-fashion.webp)

- **Height:** `min-height: 100vh` (desktop) · `min-height: 100svh` (mobile)
- **Signature component:** Ultra-condensed bold typography layered behind cursive script accent, over full-bleed editorial photography.
- **Patterns:** Minimal utility bar (`0.85rem`, `font-weight: 500`). Centered geometric navigation indicator. Pure color contrast, no gradient washes.
- **When to use:** Fashion, consumer goods, retail.

## 5. Stepped Bento Process Flow

![Stepped Bento Process Flow](../resources/design-references/section-process-stepped-cards.webp)

- **Height:** `min-height: 130vh` (desktop) · `min-height: auto` (mobile)
- **Signature component:** Diagonal workflow cards (01 Research, 02 Design, 03 Implement) cascading across viewport with client logo marquee.
- **Patterns:** Obsidian background (`#0a0a0c`), solid card fills (`#111216`), 1px borders. Number badges (`border-radius: 4px`). Thumbnail gallery integration.
- **When to use:** SaaS workflow explanations, service agency walkthroughs.

## 6. Wireframe Grid & Minimalist Staging

![Wireframe Grid & Minimalist Staging](../resources/design-references/section-editorial-wireframe-matrix.webp)

- **Height:** `min-height: 120–140vh` (desktop) · `min-height: auto` (mobile)
- **Signature component:** Monochromatic wireframe grid with floating portrait cards, bracketed micro-tags, and clean typographic hierarchy.
- **Patterns:** Subtle grid overlay (`rgba(255,255,255,0.04)`). Minimal bracketed buttons. Asymmetric card placement on grid intersections.
- **When to use:** Editorial websites, healthcare platforms, legal counsel, publications.

## 7. Multi-Stage Metrics & Timeline

![Multi-Stage Metrics & Timeline](../resources/design-references/section-metrics-timeline-multistage.webp)

- **Height:** `min-height: 140vh` (desktop) · `min-height: auto` (mobile)
- **Signature component:** Triple-stage deck: demographic metric cards, horizontal interactive timeline with pill nodes, and circular sponsor stage with date markers.
- **Patterns:** Deep crimson and black palette. Horizontal timeline scrubbers that stack on mobile. Circular avatar clusters.
- **When to use:** Event landing pages, conference schedules, investor decks, analytics.

## 8. Swiss International Split Stage

![Swiss International Split Stage](../resources/design-references/section-hero-swiss-minimalism.webp)

- **Height:** `min-height: 100vh` (desktop) · `min-height: 100svh` (mobile)
- **Signature component:** 50/50 vertical split — monumental grotesque typography on white upper half, warm photography container with rounded corners below.
- **Patterns:** Extreme typographic hierarchy and intentional whitespace. `border-radius: 12px` on image container. Lowercase branding.

## 9. Layered Cards & Horizontal Gallery

![Layered Cards & Horizontal Gallery](../resources/design-references/section-gallery-horizontal-carousel.webp)

- **Height:** `min-height: 120–130vh` (desktop) · `min-height: auto` (mobile)
- **Signature component:** Dark mode hero with 3D layered tilt cards behind display typography, transitioning to horizontal photo strip with directional controls.
- **Patterns:** Layered card fan-out on interaction. Numbered section prefix (`08 GALLERY`). Card metadata captions.
- **When to use:** Creator portfolios, photo galleries, case study carousels.

## Review Checklist

When building any section, verify:

- [ ] The section is a complete, self-contained visual experience.
- [ ] It includes a distinct signature component (not just text bullets).
- [ ] Desktop height uses deliberate vertical scale (`100–140vh`).
- [ ] Mobile gracefully adapts with `min-height: auto` and stacked layouts.
- [ ] Aesthetic integrity: solid colors, clean typography, minimal radius.
